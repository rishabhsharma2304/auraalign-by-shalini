About Shalini Chaudhary

An engineer by training, an astrologer by calling — here to help you align your energy and move forward with clarity.

My journey to astrology did not begin in a temple courtyard — it began in lecture halls and server rooms. I am Shalini Chaudhary, and for years I lived the life of an IT professional, completing my M.Tech in Information Technology and working in a field that prizes logic, structure and proof. That training never left me; it simply found a new purpose.

Astrology came to me as a calling rather than a career move. The more I studied the old texts, the more I recognised something familiar — patterns, systems, cause and effect. Where some see mysticism, I saw a structured language for understanding human life. I chose to follow that pull, and I have never looked back.

Today, my work sits at the meeting point of two worlds. The analytical mind asks why and tests every claim; the intuitive practice listens for what the chart is quietly saying. Together they keep me honest. I won't dress up a vague prediction as certainty, and I won't reduce a living person to a fixed fate. A birth chart, to me, describes tendencies and timing — not a sentence already written.

Because no single discipline answers every question, I trained across several. Vedic astrology gives the foundational map. The KP system sharpens it for precise, event-focused timing. Numerology reveals the rhythm in names and dates. Vastu addresses the energy of the spaces we live and work in. And tarot offers a focused, intuitive lens when a specific decision needs clarity. Drawing on all of these, I can look at a question from more than one angle before offering guidance.

This is the heart of AuraAlign: the belief that astrology is a tool for empowerment and alignment, not fear or fatalism. My remedies are practical and livable — small, meaningful adjustments rather than expensive rituals or scare tactics. The goal is always the same: to help you understand your moment clearly and act on it with confidence.

Above all, I hold every consultation as a private, respectful conversation. Whether we speak in Hindi or English, you'll find a space free of judgement, where your concerns are met with both rigour and warmth.
