Vedic Astrology

Vedic astrology begins with your birth chart — the Kundali — a precise map of the sky at the moment you were born. By studying the placement of planets across houses and signs, your running dashas (planetary periods) and the current transits, I help you understand the timing and texture of the season you're in.

This is the foundation of everything I do: a grounded, big-picture reading of career, relationships, health and finances, paired with clear guidance on how to make the most of supportive periods and steady yourself through challenging ones.

What's included

  • Full birth-chart (Kundali) analysis
  • Dasha & transit timeline
  • Career, relationship & health guidance
  • Practical, livable remedies

Typical duration: 45–60 minutes

KP System

Krishnamurti Paddhati (KP) is a modern, highly precise school of astrology built for clear yes-or-no, when-will-it-happen questions. Instead of broad strokes, it uses the sub-lord system to pinpoint specific events and their timing with remarkable accuracy.

If you have a focused question — "Will this job come through?", "When is the right window to buy a home?", "Is this the time to marry?" — the KP method lets me give you a sharp, event-focused answer rather than a general outlook.

What's included

  • Sub-lord based event analysis
  • Precise timing windows
  • Focused, question-specific answers
  • Clear next-step guidance

Typical duration: 30–45 minutes

Advanced Numerology

Numbers carry rhythm. Your date of birth and the spelling of your name generate a personal numerical signature that influences how opportunities and obstacles tend to show up in your life. Advanced numerology reads this signature in depth — well beyond a single "life-path number."

I'll map your core numbers, identify the years and dates that work in your favour, and flag the patterns worth being mindful of — so you can plan launches, decisions and important events with the calendar on your side.

What's included

  • Life-path & core number analysis
  • Name & date compatibility
  • Lucky numbers & favourable timing
  • Guidance for big decisions

Typical duration: 30–45 minutes

Advanced Vastu

The spaces we live and work in carry their own energy, and small misalignments can quietly drain harmony, focus or prosperity. Advanced Vastu examines the directions, layout and flow of your home or workplace to bring it back into balance.

My approach is firmly practical. Rather than demanding structural demolition, I focus on realistic, livable corrections — placement, colours, arrangement and simple adjustments — that respect your actual space and budget while shifting the energy in your favour.

What's included

  • Directional & layout assessment
  • Home or workplace energy review
  • No-demolition, livable remedies
  • Placement, colour & arrangement tips

Typical duration: 45–60 minutes
